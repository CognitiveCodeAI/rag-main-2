# Research Methodology

## Background

Prior research in this field has established baseline measurements. Our work builds upon the Smith et al. (2022) framework.

## Data Collection

We collected samples from 500 participants over a 6-month period. Participants were selected using stratified random sampling to ensure demographic representation.

## Analysis Methods

The primary analysis used logistic regression with the following variables:
- Age (continuous)
- Income bracket (categorical)
- Geographic region (categorical)
- Prior purchase history (continuous)

Statistical significance was set at p < 0.05 with Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons.

## Validation

Results were validated using k-fold cross-validation with k=10. The model achieved an AUC of 0.87 on the held-out test set.

## Limitations

Several limitations should be noted. First, the sample was limited to urban areas. Second, the 6-month timeframe may not capture seasonal variations adequately.
