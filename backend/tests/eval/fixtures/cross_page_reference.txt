# Detailed Analysis

The quarterly results reveal several important patterns.

Referring back to Figure 3.1, we can see that Q3 showed the largest year-over-year improvement at 23%. This aligns with our summer marketing campaign investment.

The Q4 results were particularly strong. As illustrated in Figure 3.1, the holiday season revenue exceeded projections by 18%.

## Contributing Factors

Several factors contributed to the growth shown in Figure 3.1:

1. Expanded digital marketing reach
2. New product launches in Q2
3. Improved supply chain efficiency
