# Revenue Analysis

The following table summarizes quarterly revenue across regions.

Table 2: Regional Revenue Q1-Q4 2024 (in millions USD)

| Region    | Q1    | Q2    | Q3    | Q4    | Total  |
|-----------|-------|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| North     | 125   | 142   | 158   | 189   | 614    |
| South     | 98    | 105   | 112   | 145   | 460    |
| East      | 87    | 91    | 102   | 118   | 398    |
| West      | 156   | 178   | 195   | 221   | 750    |

Table 2 shows that the Western region consistently outperformed other regions throughout the year. The growth trajectory in Q4 indicates strong holiday season performance.

The North region showed steady improvement, with a particularly strong Q4 that exceeded expectations by 15%.

## Analysis

Looking at Table 2, we can identify several trends:

1. Western region dominance
2. Q4 surge across all regions
3. Eastern region needs attention
